EMBER
DUEL

A card battler of ember and elements.
Outwit Kindle, the rival who always tells you what she plans next.

▲ Flame — burns ● Tide — flows ◆ Leaf — mends ■ Stone — endures

Goal: reduce Kindle's life from 20 to 0 before she does the same to you.

Your turn: you gain ember (mana) and draw a card. Click a card — or press 1–5 — to play it if you can afford its ember cost. Press Enter or Space to end your turn.

Surge combo: play two cards of the same element in one turn to trigger that element's surge — Flame +3 damage, Tide draws a card, Leaf heals 3, Stone gains 4 shield.

Read Kindle: her banner tells you her intent for her next turn — gathering embers, raising guard or stoking flames — so you can shield up or strike first.

Shield absorbs damage until your next turn. Heal restores life. If your deck runs out, it reshuffles from your discard pile. You start with a head start of 1 extra ember.

Ember storm: from turn 13 on, the table itself burns both duelists a little more each round — so no duel can stall forever.
